Three behaviours are deliberate and can look like defects on a fast pass. Please check these first.

1. After three correct seats the home rail shows "Rail settled." and "Those three left for Saved. Spread the next trio." That is the intended end of a trio, not a crash. Use "Spread next" when three more works wait.
2. "Spread the rail" or "Spread next" with fewer than three waiting works leaves or returns the rail to "Rail bare." with no extra alert. Save until three works wait, then spread again.
3. Tapping a "Hooked" or "Settled" canvas opens Saved instead of lifting it. Only a "Waiting" canvas lifts; tap that seat, or use "Seat this painting", to continue.
