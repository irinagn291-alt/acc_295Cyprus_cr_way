This is version 1.0, the first release of CR Way.

CR Way is a Philadelphia Museum of Art matching rail. You save paintings from Explore, then hang each waiting canvas under the nameplate that names it, by maker or by title.

This release includes Explore with Maker or title search, Save, Keep, and a local shelf of eight paintings; a home rail to spread three waiting works and seat them under nameplates; Seat this painting, Recent hooks, Drops on Saved, and Retract newest mark; Saved sections Seated, Hooks, and Drops; Settings with Philadelphia Museum of Art, Open access credit, Contact, Walk the pages again, and Erase the rail; three onboarding pages Seat each canvas, Lift, then hook, and Keep the rail; and Shortcuts Open Quiz, Open Explore, Open Saved, Open Settings, Spread the rail, Lift a canvas, and Hook a nameplate.

English only. Portrait and light appearance. iPhone and iPad. Minimum iOS 17.0. Saved works, hooks, and drops stay on this device.
