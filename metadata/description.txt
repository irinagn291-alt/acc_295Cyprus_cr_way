CR Way is a Philadelphia Museum of Art matching rail for people who want that match on this device, not a museum walk, a shop, or an account. You save paintings, then hang each waiting canvas under the nameplate that names it, by maker or by title.

• Save Philadelphia Museum of Art paintings from Explore, including a local shelf when search is quiet or fails.

• Explore keeps a local shelf of 26 Philadelphia Museum of Art paintings. Each row shows the year and a short note, including The Gross Clinic by Thomas Eakins, Nude Descending a Staircase No. 2 by Marcel Duchamp, and further works by Monet, Cassatt, Poussin, and others.
• After three saves, home shows Three works are waiting. Choose Makers or Titles, then Spread. The same Spread sits at the top of Explore.
